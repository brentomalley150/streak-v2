# Toast
A brief confirmation at the bottom of the screen after an action, gone after 2.4 seconds.

**Markup:** `<div class="toast" role="status">` with one short line. It's fixed to the bottom, 440px max.

- Confirms what just happened, in the kid's terms: "Logged for Yesterday", "🌟 Perfect day! +2 bonus points", "Drums — same points, new practice ideas".
- `green` text and border. On the parent surface that's 3.77:1 — keep toasts on the kid surface or use `green-text`.
